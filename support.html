Life in the UK Test Prep

An iPhone app for the Life in the UK test: 541 questions written from the official handbook, 21 mock tests scored the way the real one is, and a 30-day plan of daily classes.

Getting Help

Email uktest@codesplitters.com with any question, problem, or suggestion. If you think a question in the app is wrong, please say which one and what you believe the correct answer is — it will be checked against the handbook.

Common Questions

I missed a day of the plan. Have I lost it?
No. The plan waits where you left it. Come back in a week and you pick up on the same day you left, not further along.

Can I read ahead?
Yes. Tap any day in the plan to read its notes early. The classes and checks stay shut until you reach that day, so the material is still taught in order.

How do I start the plan over?
In the plan, tap the menu at the top right and choose Reset the plan. Your test history and weak spots are kept.

How do I clear everything for someone else to use the app?
Settings → Reset all progress. It clears every test, every answer, your weak spots, the 30-day plan and your study decks, as if the app were new.

Where do the questions come from?
Every question is written from Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents, third edition, the official handbook the real test is based on.
