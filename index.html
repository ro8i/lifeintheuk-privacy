Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Life in the UK Test Prep ("the App") is developed by Code Splitters. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. Everything it remembers — your answers, test results, weak spots, how far you have got through the 30-day plan, and your settings — is stored on your own device only.

No Internet Connection

The App contains no networking code at all. It makes no requests of any kind, and works entirely offline.

No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account or log in. There is no registration process.

No Analytics or Tracking

The App does not use any analytics tools, tracking software, advertising frameworks, or third-party software development kits.

No Advertising

The App does not display any advertisements.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the App schedules them with iOS on your device. They are created and delivered locally; nothing about them leaves your phone. You can turn them off at any time in the App's settings, or in the iOS Settings app.

Deleting Your Data

Settings → Reset all progress clears every test, every answer, your weak spots, the 30-day plan and your study decks. Deleting the App removes everything else with it. Because nothing is stored anywhere but your device, there is nothing left behind for us to delete.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Not an Official App

The App is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Home Office, UK Visas and Immigration, or any UK government body. The official guidance on the Life in the UK test is at gov.uk.

Contact
